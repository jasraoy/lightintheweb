MY COLLEGE JOURNEY
01 — LEARNING & GROWING

My Journey as a Computer Science Student

Being a first-year Computer Science student has introduced me to a new world of learning and challenges. It is my first step into understanding how technology works, how programs are created, and how ideas can be turned into something useful.

Along the way, I have started learning programming, particularly C++, as well as the basics of HTML and CSS. There are times when coding feels confusing, and some activities require patience and practice. But every error I encounter teaches me something new.

I know that I am still at the beginning of this journey. I may not know everything yet, but I am excited to keep learning, improving my skills, and discovering what I can achieve as a future Computer Science professional.

MY SOURCE OF STRENGTH
02 — FAITH & INSPIRATION

A Verse That Keeps Me Going

"When the time is right, I, the Lord, will make it happen." ISAIAH 60:22

This verse holds a special meaning for me because it reminds me that everything has its own time. Sometimes, I find myself worrying about my future, questioning my progress, or wondering if I am doing enough to reach my dreams.

As a first-year college student, I know that I still have a long way to go. There will be challenges, unexpected changes, and moments when things do not happen the way I planned. But this verse reminds me that I do not need to rush everything or compare my journey with others.

I believe that God knows what is best for me and that His timing is always worth trusting. For now, my part is to keep learning, working hard, praying, and doing my best with what I have.

I may not see everything falling into place today, but I trust that when the right time comes, God will make a way. Until then, I will continue growing, believing, and trusting His plans.
